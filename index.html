The problem

Buying second-hand runs on trust, and trust is broken

India's $50B pre-owned market is full of unverified claims, hidden defects and fake items.

70%+

Buyers hesitate

Severe information asymmetry: unverified seller claims, hidden defects and fake items scare buyers away.

20–30%

"Lemon discount"

Honest sellers lose value because buyers pre-emptively discount offers to cover defect risk.

30–50%

Buyback deductions

Buyback hubs mark items down heavily, while classifieds expose users to advance-payment and QR-code scams.

💚 Social need

Students and budget-conscious households need affordable pre-owned goods but fear scams. Honest sellers lose value to defect-risk discounts. Everyone deserves a safe way to reuse.

How it works

From listing to a safe handover in 4 steps

A Flutter + Node.js app that uses computer vision to verify every item before it's sold.

📸 List the item

The seller snaps photos and adds category, price and location. AI suggests listing text and a fair price.

🔍 Scan & verify

A guided camera frame lets computer vision find scratches, dents and wear.

Technology · C-BOM

Built on a proven, scalable stack

📱 Apps

Flutter mobile (Android + iOS) and Flutter Web, responsive.

⚙️ Backend

Node.js REST APIs on AWS EC2 t3.small.

🗄️ Data

PostgreSQL on AWS RDS, S3 (50 GB) + CloudFront CDN.

👁️ Vision AI

OpenCV + YOLOv8 defect detection and 1–10 scoring.

✨ AI assistant

OpenAI API for listing text and price suggestions.

💳 Payments

Razorpay escrow with a 24-hour hold.

🗺️ Location

Google Maps API for local search within 15 km.

🔔 Comms

Firebase, SMS/OTP and AWS SES.

🔬 Exploratory: 3D LiDAR & repair circularity

TrueDepth and LiDAR sensors capture point clouds to measure dent depth and frame warping against CAD references. Partnerships with local 3D-print hubs could replace broken covers, clips and battery doors on demand (PETG/TPU).
